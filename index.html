Amenities & Appliances

🛁Whirlpool bathtubPozzi BN-416 · follow steps 1 to 5 in order

Never run the jets dryWater must cover every jet plus 2–3 in (5–8 cm) above the highest one before you press ON. A dry pump burns out.

  1. PrepareTurn the chrome drain knob to close the drain. Clear hair and debris from the jets and overflow.
  2. FillSet the Diverter to tub fill. Open Cold, then Hot. Aim for 38–40 °C, never hotter. Fill past the water line above, then close Hot, then Cold.
  3. Start the jetsDry your hands. Press POWER ON/OFF once = ON, again = OFF. Twist a nozzle ring to adjust force and swivel it to aim.
  4. Hand showerTurn the Diverter to the shower position. Test the temperature on your hand first. Return the wand, then set the Diverter back to tub fill.
  5. Finish & drainJets OFF first: press POWER ON/OFF. Then turn the chrome drain knob to open the drain. Rinse jets and walls. Close all faucets.

Close all faucetsTurn Hot, Cold and Diverter fully clockwise until they stop. Check that nothing drips.

Before you leave, check all 4

  • ✔ All knobs closedHot, Cold and Diverter turned fully clockwise
  • ✔ Jets turned offJets are silent
  • ✔ Drain closedSet to closed after the tub is empty
  • ✔ Lights offSwitch off the bathroom lights
🎮PS5 safe shutdownProtect your software from corruption

Never use the console buttons or pull the cableThe PS5 only shuts down safely if software can close properly. Pulling the plug causes data corruption. Be patient!

  1. PS buttonPress the PlayStation button on your DualSense. Do not power off yet.
  2. Select the power iconGo to the lower quick menu, select the power icon, and choose Turn Off PS5.
  3. Wait for the LEDThe blue light bar will flash. It is only safe when it turns off completely.
  4. Avoid console buttonsNever use the physical power or disc eject buttons to turn off, and don't unplug. It is an unsafe shutdown method.
